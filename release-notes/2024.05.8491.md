# 2024.05.8491

## New features

- `run_zcfd -v` prints the zCFD version, and `run_zcfd -o <n>` sets the OpenMP threads per process.
- Acoustic observers can record over a chosen window with `observerStartTime` and `observerEndTime`.

## Changes in behaviour

- The acoustic solver settings require `c`, `dt`, `rho0` and `p0`. Remove any per-surface `startTimeI` and `endTimeI` keys.
- Under Slurm, `run_zcfd` limits OpenMP threads to the job's allocation.

## Fixes

- Improved robustness of acoustic workflows.
