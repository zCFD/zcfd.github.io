# 2023.01.3113

## New features

- A farfield initial condition can be a driving function, re-evaluated at each report, so a run can steer itself towards a target such as a lift coefficient and stop when it is reached.
- A pressure inflow can take an optional `mach`.
- The acoustic solver offers `multi level` time marching on Cartesian meshes and further FRPM source controls.
- The Fluent converter accepts internal boundary zones and unrecognised boundary types.

## Changes in behaviour

- Validation requires reports of each kind to have unique names, and rejects periodic boundary conditions with `implicit euler` time marching.
- A pressure outflow must set a static pressure ratio.

## Fixes

- Improved robustness of overset and rotating-mesh workflows.

## Platforms and packaging

- ParaView 5.10.1 is bundled.
