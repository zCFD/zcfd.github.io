# 2022.05.1788

## New features

- Added the `"Roe"` and `"Roe low diss"` inviscid flux schemes, recommended for implicit runs at large CFL. The inviscid flux scheme can also be chosen for LES runs.
- The `implicit euler` scheme supports turbulence models, sponge layers, moving meshes and overset boundaries.
- The incompressible solver supports unsteady runs and an energy equation, and writes `yplus` and skin friction.
- Added sliding meshes (`'sliding planes': True`) and accelerating translating meshes (`translation function`).
- New run controls: `first order cycles`, `number of additional overset active layers` and `cycles per overset cycle`.
- `fwensight` output includes boundary data, and `tauconvert` converts TAU meshes.

## Fixes

- Improved robustness of restart, overset and acoustic workflows.
