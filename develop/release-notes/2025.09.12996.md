# 2025.09.12996

## New features

- In-situ visualisation uses ParaView Catalyst 2.
- zutil adds acoustic post-processing: A-weighting, overall sound pressure level and spectrum plots.

## Changes in behaviour

- `validate_input` normalises a rotation `axis` that is not a unit vector, with a warning.

## Validation cases

- Added the Windsor square-back body for the incompressible solver, compared with wind-tunnel data.

## Platforms and packaging

- ParaView 6.0 is bundled, and the bundled Python is 3.12.
