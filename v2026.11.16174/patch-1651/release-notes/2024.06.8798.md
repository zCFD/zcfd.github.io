# 2024.06.8798

## New features

- Forces can be reported on immersed walls, and immersed-boundary solutions can be written on STL surfaces.
- Force reports in a rotating reference frame include thrust along the rotation axis.

## Changes in behaviour

- Actuator-disc fluid zones (`type: "disc"`) are defined through `controller`, `geometry`, `discretisation` and `model` blocks and require `name` and `rotation direction`. Rewrite decks that use the previous flat keys. The turbine and propeller utilities have moved to `zutil.zWind`.
- `immersed boundary surface` in `write output` takes a list of `zone` and `stl` pairs; replace `immersed boundary surface file` with it.
- `sponge layer damping` requires `distance`, `damping factor` and `condition`.

## Fixes

- Improved robustness of overset, ParaView reader and cluster launch workflows.
